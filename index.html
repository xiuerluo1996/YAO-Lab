Decoding the molecular world with mass spectrometry

We develop new mass-spectrometry-based methods to study protein dynamics, store data in molecules, discover peptide drugs and biomarkers, and ensure food safety.

Representative work

Selected highlights

Protein-based digital data storage

The first complete storage-to-retrieval cycle in de novo designed unnatural proteins, achieving ~30× density at 10% of the cost — published in Nature Communications.

Rapid edible-oil authentication

MALDI-MS methods for fast, accurate classification and quantification of edible oils, screening adulterated and recycled ("gutter") oils.

Protein conformational dynamics

Native MS, hydrogen/deuterium exchange MS and ion mobility MS to reveal the dynamics of β-lactamases, SARS-CoV-2 proteins and their interactions.

Ambient ionization techniques

Pioneering wooden-tip electrospray ionization and other substrate-based ionization methods for direct, in situ analysis.
