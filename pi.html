Research interests

Prof. Yao leads an interdisciplinary team at the interface of mass spectrometry, analytical chemistry, chemical biology and multi-omics, applying MS-based methods to chemistry, biology, medicine, food science and information science.

• Molecular data storage

• Conformational dynamics of proteins & interactions

• Peptide drug discovery

• Disease mechanism & biomarker discovery

• Molecular assembly & recognition

• Food safety & authentication

• New analytical techniques
